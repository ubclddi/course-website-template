# Course Content

## Course Topics

1. A starting place for GenAI: Introductions, Ideas, and Experimentation (IP 1, Required)
2. Critically evaluating the use(s) of GenAI in Learning, Teaching, and the Workplace (IP 2, Required)
3. Technology and Media analysis in historical perspective
4. GenAI in Popular and Public Media
5. Understanding Institutional Policies and Guidelines for GenAI
6. GenAI, Histories, Refusals, and Futures
7. Power, Politics, and Ecologies
8. Conscious and Feeling Machines
9. GenAI, Information worlds, (post) truth, and p(doom).
10. Literacy, Learning, and Cognitive Friction (IP 10, Required)

## Readings and Resources
